Delete a StudyMate account

You can delete your StudyMate account and the data stored for it from inside the app:

  1. Sign in to StudyMate.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

The app removes the local account and planner records. If Firebase is configured, it also removes the account's Firestore profile and uploaded assignment files. Connect to the internet first so the cloud deletion can finish.

If you cannot access the app, request deletion using the developer support email shown in the StudyMate Google Play listing. Include the account email you used in StudyMate. Do not post account details publicly.

See the StudyMate privacy policy for details about stored data.
